# Phase 2 source verification: ServiceM8 Diary and construction companies

Research date: 19 October 2026<br>
Status: Primary-source desk research; no Royal Glass live UI/API parity test<br>
Scope: Read-only Job history and a one-URL background report about a **construction-related company**. A person is not a report subject. Director names may be included only as company-register facts, without personal background investigation.

## Decision summary

1. ServiceM8 publishes separate Job, note, email, SMS, schedule, attachment, form, payment and Inbox read surfaces. This establishes possible sources, **not** that every Royal Glass Job Diary item is retrievable or that the Office Agent currently has the scopes. The Phase 2 Job feature gate remains a UI/API parity audit. [ServiceM8 authentication and scope table](https://developer.servicem8.com/docs/authentication), [ServiceM8 Jobs](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listjobs).
2. The company report can proceed on its own release path. Prefer the official NZBN API for automated entity matching and the Companies Register for a checked company record. Access requires an approved API subscription/key. [Companies Office API guidance](https://companies-register.companiesoffice.govt.nz/help-centre/getting-support-to-use-the-companies-register/searching-using-other-applications/), [NZBN API](https://portal.api.business.govt.nz/api/nzbn).
3. A public project photo is not automatically reusable: preserve the source link and attribution, and obtain a licence or permission before copying the image into a stored report or marketing material. [IPONZ permission guidance](https://www.iponz.govt.nz/get-ip/copyright/getting-permission/).

## 1. ServiceM8 Job Diary read-source map

| Requested item | Documented read surface and scope | What remains unproved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job created/current fields | [Job list/read](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listjobs), `read_jobs`, exposes generated ID, `created_by_staff_uuid`, `date`, address and current state. | `date` is described as created **or scheduled**; do not treat it as an exact creation timestamp. A [`job.created` event webhook](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/post_event_webhook_subscription) exists for future events but does not establish historic event replay. Compare the UI's created item to available fields. |
| Notes | [Note list](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listnotes), `read_job_notes`, with Job filtering. | Full text, author/time semantics and every UI note need an account test. |
| Job emails and customer email replies | [Merged inbound/outbound Job email list](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listemailmessages) and [single email](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/getemailmessage), `read_email`; list accepts direction/related Job filters and cursor pagination. | The public list page does not establish that all body, attachment, thread/reply-link and delivery fields needed by the product are present. Test both directions and link to Inbox/Job Diary items; do not infer a customer reply from matching addresses alone. |
| Job SMS and customer SMS replies | [Merged inbound/outbound Job SMS](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listsmsmessages), `read_sms`, has direction, time, message, Job UUID and cursor. | Reconcile against the UI, including replies and messages before/after an Inbox-to-Job conversion. |
| Inbox enquiry and calls | [Inbox list](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listinboxmessages), `read_inbox`, has message type including `phone_call`, message content and `converted_to_job_uuid`; [detail read](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/getinboxmessage) documents attachments/conversation history. | `phone_call` in Inbox does not prove all Job Diary call entries, recordings or transcripts are available. Verify the specific Royal Glass calls and whether conversion preserves the relevant link. |
| Bookings, visits and allocations | [Job Activity](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listjobactivities) and [Job Allocation](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listjoballocations), `read_schedule`. | Test whether the endpoints expose past changes and cancellations or only current records; reconcile UI booking events and staff assignments. |
| Quotes | [Job](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listjobs) has quote sent flag/stamp; [Attachment](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listattachments), `read_attachments`, identifies sources such as `QUOTE`; [binary download](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/downloadattachmentfile) reads files. | Quote version, status/acceptance, amount and sent-document association require live proof. A quote-sent stamp alone does not prove no reply or acceptance. |
| Files and photos | [Attachment metadata](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listattachments), `read_attachments`, includes related object, type, source, timestamp and extracted metadata; [download](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/downloadattachmentfile) returns a temporary redirect. [Scopes](https://developer.servicem8.com/docs/authentication) also list `read_job_photos` and `read_job_attachments`. | Compare which scopes and endpoints cover each UI photo/file, whether file bytes can be read, and extraction/vision quality. Do not forward the ServiceM8 credential to the redirected storage host. |
| Forms | [Form Response list](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listformresponses), `read_forms`, links to a Job and includes `field_data`, timestamp and generated document attachment UUID. | Test which completed forms appear in the Diary and whether answers/PDFs match. |
| Invoices and payments | [Job](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listjobs), `read_jobs`, exposes invoice sent/date and full-payment flag; [Job Payment list](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listjobpayments), `read_job_payments`, contains payment amount/time and deposit flag; invoice file may be an [Attachment](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listattachments). | `invoice_date` is the issue date used with client terms, **not an explicit due date**. Verify actual due date, balance, void/reissue and partial payment before any overdue flag. |
| Status/queue changes and other UI items | [Job](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listjobs) has current status, queue and some state timestamps; [event webhook list](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/post_event_webhook_subscription) includes status/queue/quote/invoice events for subscriptions. | Neither current fields nor future webhooks establish a complete historic event stream. Catalogue every UI Diary type and find a historic read source or keep the Job release gated. |

ServiceM8's [pagination guide](https://developer.servicem8.com/docs/pagination) requires walking the `x-next-cursor` until absent on cursor-paginated endpoints. The [Inbox list](https://developer.servicem8.com/reference/listinboxmessages) instead documents offset/limit/`has_more`; adapters must follow each endpoint's actual contract. A failed page or denied scope is incomplete coverage, not an empty history.

### Royal Glass proof still required

The [Job Context completeness audit](../audits/2026-10-27-job-context-verification.md) records why the current source set does not prove full OAuth reads or UI parity. The present research made **no** live ServiceM8 calls. Before releasing any new Phase 2 Job feature, compare a specifically authorised set of Jobs in the ServiceM8 UI against all granted read endpoints. For each visible item record type, count, UI timestamp, API ID/link, full-content availability, duplicates and omissions. Include at least one inbound/outbound email, reply, SMS, call, booking change, quote/version, invoice/payment, photo/file and form. Any relevant UI-visible item without an approved read path keeps the Job feature gate closed under [ADR 0004](../adr/0004-hold-phase-2-job-features-for-diary-coverage.md).

## 2. One-URL construction-company report: permitted source map

| Source | Verified use and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Company or project website supplied by staff | Use the page as a lead to identify a **construction-related business**, its services and claimed projects. Verify the legal entity before attributing register facts. Website text and images are publisher claims, not independent quality evidence. Check the site's access terms, copyright and robots policy for each domain before automated retrieval or persistent copying; there is no blanket permission for arbitrary sites. [IPONZ website-copyright guidance](https://www.iponz.govt.nz/get-ip/copyright/finding-copyright-owners/). |
| NZ Companies Register | Official search supports name, company number or NZBN and shows incorporation date, status, registered office, directors and filings. Use these as company facts with retrieval date; do not expand into personal profiles, home addresses or personal checks. A similarly named company is not an identity match. [Companies Register search guide](https://companies-register.companiesoffice.govt.nz/help-centre/getting-support-to-use-the-companies-register/searching-the-companies-register/). |
| NZBN API | Companies Office recommends this route for application/large-volume searches. Public Primary Business Data may include trading names, industry, trading area and website, but optional fields can be absent/private. Subscription approval and a key are required; sandbox data is not production-matched. [Companies Office API guidance](https://companies-register.companiesoffice.govt.nz/help-centre/getting-support-to-use-the-companies-register/searching-using-other-applications/), [NZBN API](https://portal.api.business.govt.nz/api/nzbn). |
| LINZ addresses, parcels and aerial context | [NZ Addresses](https://data.linz.govt.nz/layer/123113-nz-addresses/) is CC BY 4.0, explicitly permits commercial use with attribution, and offers APIs. [LINZ Data Service](https://www.linz.govt.nz/products-services/data/linz-data-service) includes property/boundary and aerial datasets; check each selected layer's own licence and acquisition date. An address or aerial view provides site context; it does not prove that the company performed work there. Bulk property-owner data needs a separate licence. |
| Homes.co.nz | Its [terms](https://homes.co.nz/terms) disallow automated viewing without consent and limit use to personal purposes or agents listing properties. Exclude from this automated commercial report unless permission is obtained. |
| Public project/product photos | Record the publishing page and image owner/licence. Linking to the source is the initial safe report shape; displaying via remote embed, downloading, persisting a copy, AI processing and marketing reuse each need a source-specific rights and technical decision. Credit alone does not replace permission. [IPONZ getting permission](https://www.iponz.govt.nz/get-ip/copyright/getting-permission/). |
| Public reviews | Do not treat company testimonials as independent reviews. If Google Places is chosen, use its licensed API and comply with [Places policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies): author/source attribution, direct source links and caching restrictions. Google says reviews are not verified, so show review count/date and limits rather than a definitive quality rating. Other review sites need their own terms review. |

## 3. Proposed evidence contract for the company report

One input URL (company site or public project page) should yield a company identity candidate. Confirm a construction-related company and exact entity match using NZBN/company number, official site cross-link, or staff selection when ambiguous. If no company can be confidently identified, stop at an unresolved identity report; do not attach another entity's directors or history. A project page may supply site context and linked photos, but property/home context is secondary and may be absent.

Store sourced company facts, source URLs, checked timestamps, licence/access status, and separate self-published project evidence from independent reviews. The AI can propose balustrade, pool-fencing or swimming-pool collaboration with a reason and practical approach, clearly marked as a suggestion. It cannot infer actual workmanship, payment behaviour or willingness to partner from a marketing page. The report subject is the **company**; director names are factual fields from the register, not subjects for investigation. Saved reports are dated snapshots, so reruns should make new versions and avoid portraying old facts as current.

## 4. Open gates before implementation

1. Obtain Royal Glass UI/API parity evidence and confirm the exact app OAuth grant. Document any missing historic call, status, booking or reply record and whether ServiceM8 offers an approved read path.
2. Verify email body/attachment and reply-link fields, quote versions/acceptance, invoice due-date/void/balance fields, and attachment extraction on authorised test Jobs.
3. Obtain NZBN API access and confirm its terms, quotas and fields; inspect each chosen project website's access terms and image rights before automation.
4. Pick actual LINZ aerial/parcel layers and check their individual licence, freshness and attribution. Keep property data optional in a company-focused report.
5. Decide the permitted review source/API and report storage rights. A 12-month saved report may retain links and Royal Glass's own analysis; third-party image/review content has separate storage restrictions.

## 5. Additional evidence for a useful or difficult construction partnership

The report's decision question is broader than company identity: **what work could this company bring Royal Glass, and what evidence suggests the relationship could be difficult?** [NZ Government Procurement's due-diligence guide](https://www.procurement.govt.nz/guides/guide-to-procurement/source-your-suppliers/conducting-due-diligence-checks/) recommends checking identity, financial ability, capacity, past performance, health and safety, systems, references and insurance, and verifying information from more than one source. Its [construction-specific guide](https://www.procurement.govt.nz/assets/procurement-property/documents/construction-consultancy-services/due-diligence-construction-procurement.pdf) cautions that historical financial and commercial checks cannot guarantee future performance. These are useful assessment categories, not evidence that any specific company is safe or unsafe.

| Source beyond NZBN | What it can support | Limit for the report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Companies Register](https://companies-register.companiesoffice.govt.nz/help-centre/getting-support-to-use-the-companies-register/searching-the-companies-register/) | Confirm legal name/number, incorporation and current status; inspect public filings such as annual returns and insolvency documents. | A registered status is not a credit, delivery or workmanship guarantee. Match the exact entity, not just a similar trading name. |
| [GETS completed tenders](https://www.gets.govt.nz/ExternalAwardedTenderList.htm?orderBy=date&page=1) | Public-sector award notices can show an awarded construction contract or panel appointment, with project and agency context. | An award is not proof that the work was completed well or that a supplier is a good subcontracting partner. Private projects will usually be absent. |
| [WorkSafe court summaries](https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/laws-and-regulations/prosecutions/court-summaries/) and [accepted enforceable undertakings](https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/laws-and-regulations/enforceable-undertakings/accepted-enforceable-undertakings/) | Source company-level published health-and-safety outcomes and the action taken. | [WorkSafe's publishing policy](https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/laws-and-regulations/prosecutions/publishing-policy/) says some matters are not published and prosecution cases are anonymised after five years. No search result cannot mean no safety issue; an undertaking may concern an alleged breach and must not be mislabelled a conviction. |
| [MBIE CodeMark product register](https://www.building.govt.nz/building-code-compliance/product-assurance-and-certification-schemes/codemark) | For a relevant product supplier, verify a current certificate and its exact product, method and conditions of use. | Product certification is not a general company-quality or delivery certification. The [LBP register](https://www.lbp.govt.nz/for-homeowners/) concerns individual practitioners, so it is not a company background report source unless staff separately need to verify a named responsible practitioner for a specific job. |
| Company portfolio, published case studies, project photos, references and site visits | Discover work types, scale, region, likely balustrade/pool-fencing/pool opportunities, and questions for an exploratory call. [Building Performance guidance](https://www.building.govt.nz/projects-and-consents/planning-a-successful-build/scope-and-design/choosing-the-right-people-for-your-type-of-building-work/choosing-a-building-practitioner) recommends viewing past work and speaking to previous clients about communication, reliability, workmanship and delays. | A company's own portfolio is self-published. A photo is not proof of authorship, completion or quality; references are stronger only after Royal Glass confirms the company actually did the stated work. |
| Company-provided documents and Royal Glass experience | Request current insurance, comparable references, capacity/programme, scope and variation process, health-and-safety approach, warranty/rework arrangements, then compare with Royal Glass's own recorded experience. [Government Procurement](https://www.procurement.govt.nz/guides/guide-to-procurement/source-your-suppliers/conducting-due-diligence-checks/) lists insurance, client interviews, performance reports and site visits as due-diligence evidence. | These are follow-up checks, not facts a single public URL can reliably provide. Do not silently mix unverified claims or internal experience into public-source findings. |

The report should therefore have **Potential fit**, **Potential friction**, and **Questions to resolve** sections, each with specific evidence and limits. An AI suggestion can recommend a reference call, sample scope discussion or small trial project; staff make the decision. Avoid one numerical "headache score" and do not claim payment behaviour from a website or reviews. The owner's earlier decision deferred payment behaviour from the first report; an eventual financial or credit check would need a separately chosen, authorised source and terms.

## 6. Google Search as discovery, not proof

Manual Google Search can help staff discover company project pages, awards, coverage, reviews and variants of a legal or trading name. For every relevant result the agent must open the publisher's original page, check the exact company match and date, and cite that page rather than a search snippet. [Google explains](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/how-search-works) that indexing is not guaranteed, so no result cannot establish a clean record. Search results and Google Maps reviews are different products: [Places API review content](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies) has attribution and storage restrictions.

For an automated one-URL plugin, do not design around Google's old [Custom Search JSON API](https://developers.google.com/custom-search/v1/overview): Google says it is closed to new customers and existing users must transition by 1 January 2027. Select and validate a supported licensed web-search provider before implementation, and still verify discoveries against the original sources. This is an integration decision, not a reason to omit web discovery from the report.
